# Gym Tracker

A mobile web app for logging strength workouts. Pick a day, add exercises, record sets, and track progress over time — all in a simple interface that feels like a native app.

**Try it:** [https://evg-smir.github.io/gym-tracker/](https://evg-smir.github.io/gym-tracker/)

---

## Features

### Workout diary
Choose a day on the calendar and log your session: which exercises you did and how they went. History is saved by date, so you can always go back to past workouts.

### Exercise catalog
Exercises are grouped by muscle: arms, back, shoulders, chest, legs, and abs. Add several exercises at once. Mark movements as dumbbells (double weight) or bodyweight when needed.

### Sets: weight × reps
Add, edit, and delete sets for each exercise. Keep a clear record of load and volume without notebooks or spreadsheets.

### Progress statistics
Charts for max weight and training volume. For bodyweight exercises, the app shows total reps instead. Easy to see whether results are improving.

### Account
Sign up and sign in with email. Update your name, email, and password in the profile.

### Works on your phone
The app is a PWA — install it to the home screen and use it fullscreen. Data syncs to the cloud and stays available offline, so a workout is not blocked by a weak connection.
